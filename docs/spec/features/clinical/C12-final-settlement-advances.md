# C12 Final Settlement and Advances

## Purpose
Inpatients pay deposits (advances) during the stay; at discharge all charges are totalled, discounts and panel portions applied, advances adjusted, and the patient pays the balance or receives a refund. Final settlement closes the IPD encounter financially.

## Screens in current app
| Menu | Route | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Billing > Final Settlement (v2) | /sale/final-settlement-v2 | Patient search; "is_hold" filter; columns Generated By, Base Price, Discount, Net amount, Cash, Receivable, Payable, Status, Posting Date |
| Reports | Patient Wise Advance Report, Advances Report, Admission and Discharge (FS Pending), Patient Admission Wise Report | C23 |

Advances are taken at the counter (C04) and listed in Sale History > Advance History; refund and cancel advance go through C05.

## Data
Final settlement: admission, charges summary (base, discounts), net, cash collected, receivable (panel or patient credit), payable (refund due), status, posting date, hold flag (patient on hold, bill not finalised). Advance: patient, admission, amount, payment mode, user, status (Active, Refunded).

Finance mapping (F04) includes: Loss Amount Against IPD Bill (expense), Recoverable Deduction Against IPD Bill, Tax Deduction Against IPD Bill (asset), advance from patients liability account.

## API endpoints observed
